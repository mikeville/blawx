feature: the heaping dome of dessert spilling over the glass's rim — I made it the single widest AND deepest mass in the whole sculpture (full 14-wide, full 6-deep at its bulge), distinctly wider than the footed glass beneath it, so the silhouette reads as a narrow-stemmed glass topped by an oversized rounded overflow.
pose: upright and centered on the vertical axis, straight-on — a parfait's identity lives entirely in its cross-section profile (flat foot, thin stem, flaring glass, overflowing dome), which is symmetric around that axis, so a centered upright pose lets the 30° iso camera read the dome's true rounded bulge from the side and its tapering oval footprint from above, instead of only a flat front silhouette.
depth plan: real proportions are a flat wide foot, a thin stem, a glass that flares from narrow to wide, and a heaping dome wider than the glass rim. Per region: foot (y0) z1-4, 6 wide; stem (y1-2) z2-3, 2 wide (thinnest part); bowl base (y3) z2-3, 4 wide widening through (y4-5) z1-4, 6-8 wide; rim (y6-7) z1-4/z0-5 mixed, 10 wide; dome lower (y8-9) z0-5, 12 wide; dome bulge (y9-10) z0-5, 14 wide (widest+deepest point); dome upper (y11-13) tapering z1-4 then z0-5, 8-12 wide; peak (y14-15) z2-3, 4-6 wide.

bounds x:1-14 y:0-15 z:0-5
