# tier2-b1 — the first FPS-tail seed batch past Tier 1

Run 2026-08-05. 25 terms, `$0` subscription subagents, one per term.
Structural results only — **nothing here has been eyeballed**, and no
judgment pass (blind panel or otherwise) was run.

## What ran

- **Terms:** entries 79–103 of `data/seed-pool/seed-list.json` — the first
  farthest-point-sampling slice past Tier 1's 79 head terms, covering radius
  0.489 → 0.544. Generated by `scripts/make-tier2-seed-prompts.ts b1`, which
  declares the slice in-repo so the run dir reproduces from the repo alone.
- **Prompt:** the regen1-amended prompt v2, extracted verbatim into
  `scripts/lib/authoring-prompt.ts` and now shared by
  `make-tier1-regen-prompts.ts` and this generator. The extraction was
  verified by regenerating the tier1-regen1 prompts and diffing against the
  packets already on disk — byte-identical.
  **Standing assumption:** the amendments carry forward ahead of Mike's
  eyeball on the regen1 winners. If that eyeball rejects them, this batch
  was authored under a brief that did not survive.
- **Protocol:** tier1 protocol unchanged — single shot + 1 deterministic
  retry (`retry-feedback.ts --max-depth=6`), `convert-response.ts`
  self-check in-agent including the median-column-depth advisory.

## Findings

- **The top-view `fillRatio` slab check took 3 of the 4 retries**
  (spareribs, rollerskates, nutcracker); the medDepth advisory took the
  fourth (phone booth, 15 → 5). AGENTS already records this check as
  advisory-in-practice but error-in-script; this batch is more evidence —
  none of the three call-1 answers was actually broken, and all three
  retries were spent re-shaping a footprint to satisfy it.
- **The depth signals are gameable by z-staggering, and the batch found
  that out.** Phone booth, tiramisu and nutcracker all cleared their check
  not by giving parts genuinely different sizes but by pushing successive
  parts to different depth lanes — plinth at the front edge, cap at the
  back. That lowers medDepth and breaks up a full-width footprint while
  producing a shape that leans backward as it rises. Two of the three then
  described their own result as a staircase or terrace, which is the exact
  wrong-gestalt read (class C/E) the blindpanel pass flagged on banana and
  dolphin. **The stat can be satisfied without the property it proxies
  for.** Worth watching on the eyeball; it may want a term-level
  constraint rather than a stricter number.
- **Floats are normal.** Mean floating voxels 32.2 across this batch vs
  **47.5** across the 50 tier1 sets — lower, not higher. (I flagged two
  individual counts as suspicious while the batch was running; measuring
  the tier1 baseline shows they are unremarkable.)
- **`landmine` is a naval mine.** My brief told the agent to build "the
  classic cartoon/naval spherical mine with spike horns," which is a sea
  mine — a landmine is a buried disc with a pressure plate. The set is
  structurally clean and reads as a recognizable object; it is the wrong
  object. My error, not the model's. Re-authoring it is a one-agent fix.

## Open, for Mike

- **Tone.** FPS maximizes dispersion, so the tail is where the odd terms
  live. This slice includes `landmine`, `bulletproof vest` and `pillbox`.
  They passed the pool's buildability filter and they cover real regions of
  noun space, but a LEGO-manual toy showing a body-armour build is a
  judgment call, not a structural one. Nothing has been dropped — say the
  word and any of them come out of the batch.
- **No panel was run.** H-PANEL keeps the blind 2-voter free-naming panel
  as the per-batch ranker; for a fresh batch there is no incumbent to rank
  against, so it would serve as a hindsight quality read the way
  `tier1-blindpanel` did. Not run here — it is a separate approved step,
  and this batch is not swapped into anything.
- **Nothing was written to KV.** These are run artifacts on disk only.
