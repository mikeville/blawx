feature: the two round ears perched on top of the head — exaggerated as thick 4-wide nubs at the head's top corners (rows0-1). At this size a teddy bear lives or dies on the ears; nothing else in this icon set has that silhouette.
pose: standing upright and facing forward, arms held slightly out from the body so the shoulder row is nearly as wide as the head+ears, legs planted apart in a symmetric stance. In the 30° iso view the barrel-shaped chest/belly (the deepest part of the sculpture) and the forward-set snout give it a huggable, rounded plush volume instead of reading as a flat cardboard cutout.
depth plan: real proportions — round ears on a round head, short thick neck, barrel chest/belly, short out-turned arms, thick stubby legs.
