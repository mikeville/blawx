feature: the two round ears perched on top of the head — exaggerated as thick 4-wide nubs at the head's top corners (rows0-1). At this size a teddy bear lives or dies on the ears; nothing else in this icon set has that silhouette.
pose: standing upright and facing forward, arms held slightly out from the body so the shoulder row is nearly as wide as the head+ears, legs planted apart in a symmetric stance. In the 30° iso view the barrel-shaped chest/belly (set back and deep) and the forward-set snout give it a huggable, rounded plush volume instead of reading as a flat cardboard cutout.
depth plan: real proportions — round ears on a round head, short thick neck, barrel chest/belly, short out-turned arms, thick stubby legs. Per-region z(depth)/y(height): ears y0-1, z3-4 (thin, back of the skull); head y2-6, z1-4 (rounds through the cheeks); snout y7, z0-2 (pushed forward, the face's leading edge); neck y8 pinched to a single z2 cell — narrow in both x and z so the head mass does not fuse into one tall slab-column with the torso; torso y9-13, z2-5 (the deepest, roundest mass, set back behind the face — pot belly); arms y9-11 at x1-2/13-14, z3 only (thin flat paddles); legs y14-15, z3-5 (shifted back under the belly, stubby).
