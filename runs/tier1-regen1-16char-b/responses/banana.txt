feature: the dramatic curved hook. A banana's identity is its crescent curve with a blunt stem cap on one end and a sharply tapered pointed tip on the other. I oversize the curve itself: it spans nearly the full 16-cell width diagonally, arcing from a low blunt stem at the left, up through a thick ground-touching belly, to a high pointed tip curling up at the right — the C-bend reads unmistakably even as a flat gray silhouette.

pose: the banana lies with its thick convex belly resting flat on the ground (the middle third) while both ends lift off the ground asymmetrically — the stem end (left) lifts only modestly to a blunt cap, the tip end (right) curls up much higher to a sharp point. This asymmetric double-lift reads as a genuine 3D bend at the 30-degree iso camera: the top view shows the footprint sweeping diagonally front-to-back as it rises rather than sitting as a flat printed curve.
